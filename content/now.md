## Where I am

I'm currently based in Chicago, Illinois. 🌭

## Work

I work at a restaurants technology startup, and I've spent the last six years working in this space (at two different companies). For most of those six years I've been in a product facing role where I was building things directly used by restaurant operators and customers (reservations, menu management tools, Kiosk self-serve ordering). In 2026, I moved to a different role where I now get to work on platform initiatives that support engineers in product facing roles. I've been excited about this new type of work, as I've wanted to transition to working on platform level things because I think some of the technical challenges in this layer can be interesting.

## Side projects

A lot of my spare attention currently goes to public transit. [Chicago Transit Alerts](https://chicagotransitalerts.app/) is a platform I built to track service quality in Chicago – it's essentially an automated system that constantly samples bus and train locations and infers buncing, ghosting, service gaps, and other issues. It also offers Bluesky bots as well. After refining this platform I extended it to Atlanta ([Atlanta Transit Alerts](https://atlantatransitalerts.app/)), another city I'm intimately familiar with. Some folks have even begun deploying the platform for other cities like [Columbus, Ohio](https://bsky.app/profile/cbusbot.bsky.social)!

Additionally, [CTA Bus Bingo](https://cailinpitt.github.io/cta-bus-bingo/) is a bus trip planner I recently built to track bus routes I've ridden and make it easier to ride ones I haven't before (I have a goal of riding every bus route in Chicago). [Briefings](https://github.com/cailinpitt/briefings) is a collection of scripts I use to print music, transit, schedule, and weather information on a receipt printer in my home.
