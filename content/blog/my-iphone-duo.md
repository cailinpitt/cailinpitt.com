I got an iPhone Duo this week! 

Like many others, I'm pretty sick of how large smartphones are these days. The iPhone 15 I've been rocking is larger than I'd like my phone to be, and I'm really bummed Apple doesn't seem interested in making Minis anymore. Foldable Android phones have always appealed to me for many reasons, the small outer displays they have being the biggest. To me, they seemed like the best way to get back to having a "small phone." However, I've been an Apple user for so many years that getting an Android phone while using a Mac and iPad seemed like such a headache, so I decided to bide my time and wait for an iPhone foldable. When Apple announced the Duo I immediately planned on pre-ordering one.

It's really cool and _weird_ and fun to use. I love the outer display and I've been using it ~80% of the time so far. The inner display is really gorgeous, and I love the matte protector it has. The Duo is a little heavy and Touch ID took a little getting used to (I haven't used an iPhone with Touch ID since the iPhone 8), but these things haven't been issues for me.

I read a lot of [articles](https://cailinpitt.com/reading/articles) on my phone every day, and the inner screen has been a lot of fun to use for it. I tend to use the inner screen for tasks where I initially start on the outer screen and go "hmm, it would be nice to be able to see/do this on a larger surface area."

A bunch of apps have already been optimized for the iPhone Duo, but a lot haven't thus far. It's a little annoying, but not a big deal to me. I expect app developers to fix these sooner rather than later, especially since Duo sales seem to have been good and a lot of people are planning to get their hands on one.

![](/images/my-iphone-duo/IMG_0027.webp)

*My girlfriend really wanted to use Google Earth on the inner display, but it's unoptimized! Hopefully Google fixes this soon.*

The price is pretty crazy. I got a Duo with 512 GB of storage, which cost $2200. You could buy an actual personal computer for cheaper than that! However, I was "lucky" enough to pay $0 for it – when I was [laid off earlier this year](https://cailinpitt.com/blog/2026/8/01/everyone-is-so-annoying-about-ai) my former employer let me keep my highly spec'd work MacBook Pro, which I traded into Apple for a gift card. That gift card completely covered the cost of my phone. I sort of feel embarrassed owning a phone this expensive, because I feel like if I didn't own it but saw someone with one I'd think they were bad with money or something. To be fair though, I likely would have still bought this phone even without a gift card.

The inner display camera is pretty bad, but it doesn't really bother me and I find the quality of images it takes to be funny. The Duo has three cameras: two selfie cameras in the outer and inner display and a main camera on the outside of the device. The main camera and outer display selfie camera are great – the inner display selfie camera uses a lot of AI upscaling (I guess to make up for the camera being low quality?) but I feel like it makes pictures look bad. I don't really see myself using this camera other than for FaceTime calls though.

![](/images/my-iphone-duo/IMG_0028.webp)

*A selfie taken by the inner display camera. If you zoom in, you can see a lot of AI upscaling and overall the quality is low*

Overall the Duo is really cool, works well for me, and I'm glad I was able to get my hands on one.
